Delete your account

You can request deletion here even if you no longer have access to the Go Melanated app.

What happens after you request deletion

  • We verify the request before deleting or anonymizing account data.
  • Your public profile and personal account data that is no longer required will be removed or anonymized.
  • Some transaction, waiver, safety, fraud-prevention, or legally required records may be retained for an appropriate period and disconnected from your public profile where appropriate.

If you can still sign in, you can also request deletion from Menu → Account → Delete Account in the app.
