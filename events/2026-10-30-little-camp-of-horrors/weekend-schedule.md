# Little Camp of Horrors 2026 — Weekend Schedule

This schedule separates guest programming from organizer production needs. Times are working targets until Jonathan and Shannette lock them.

## Thursday, October 29 — Production Day

**Purpose:** Full setup, testing, troubleshooting, and readiness.

- Early departure / arrival at venue.
- Organizer campsites set.
- Glamping packages set.
- Haunted experience installed.
- Dining and kitchen/service area set.
- Main gathering/canopy area set.
- Activity stations set.
- Check-in station set.
- Entrance, signage, decor, lighting, and photo area installed.
- Power, sound, and lighting tested.
- Haunted route tested.
- Missing-item/store runs completed.
- Final readiness walk before ending production day.

**Standard:** Friday is not an overflow setup day.

## Friday, October 30 — Arrival & Camp Kickoff

### Guest arrival / afternoon
- Check-in.
- Verify ticket/package.
- Verify waiver status.
- Issue wristband/package identifier.
- Direct to campsite/glamping.
- Guests settle in and may begin campsite decorating.
- Popcorn/light snacks if available.

### Around 6:30 PM
- Dinner service.

### Evening
- Meet and greet.
- Karaoke.
- Casual games.
- Camp social time.
- Light Halloween atmosphere.
- Continue campsite decorating as appropriate.

**Operating goal:** Organizers are hosting guests, not scrambling to finish infrastructure.

## Saturday, October 31 — Main Event Day

### 8:00–10:00 AM — Breakfast
- Two-hour service window.
- No heavy programmed activity during meal service.

### 10:00 AM–12:30 PM — Camp Morning / Free Time
Passive and self-guided options may include:
- campsite decorating
- coloring/craft table
- Halloween bingo
- lawn games
- scavenger hunt
- cards/board games
- social canopy/lounge
- walks around permitted portions of the property

**Staffing principle:** Activities in this block should require little or no active facilitation.

### 12:30–2:30 PM — Lunch
- Simple sandwich-bar style lunch.
- Flexible service window.

### 2:30–4:30 PM — Organized Camp Games
Potential programming:
- team challenges
- relay games
- family competitions
- scavenger-hunt finale
- tug-of-war
- cornhole/lawn competitions
- Halloween-themed challenges

A dedicated Activities Lead should run this block so Jonathan and Shannette can manage production/hospitality responsibilities.

### 4:30–6:00 PM — Halloween Reset / Preparation
Guests:
- change into costumes
- finish campsite decorating
- shower/reset
- relax and prepare for nighttime programming

Team:
- finish dinner
- prepare trail actors/stations
- lighting and sound check
- prepare judges and voting materials
- prepare trick-or-treat flow
- confirm haunted rotation/queue process

### 6:00 PM — Dinner
Dinner occurs before the primary nighttime sequence.

### Nightfall — Trick-or-Treat + Campsite Contest
- Guests circulate among participating campsites.
- Trick-or-treating and campsite viewing happen at the same time.
- Official judging occurs during the circuit.
- People's Choice voting can run concurrently.

### After/alongside judging — Haunted Experience Rotations
- Guests enter in controlled groups based on confirmed capacity.
- Not all attendees enter simultaneously.
- A dedicated Haunted Lead controls queue, flow, actors, and resets.

### Main Camp During Haunted Rotations
For guests waiting, returning, or opting out:
- music
- snacks
- karaoke
- Halloween games
- campfire/social area
- photo area
- low-labor party activities

### Tentative 10:00 PM–Midnight — Camp After Dark
Potential adult-focused period after the family-centered Halloween programming.

Final structure depends on number/ages of attending children, venue quiet hours, and final programming decision.

## Sunday, November 1 — Breakfast & Departure

- Breakfast.
- Guests pack campsites.
- Organized production breakdown begins.
- Equipment checked against inventory/load sheets.
- Site sweep.
- Departure.

The venue's flexible departure situation should be used to create an orderly breakdown rather than a rushed exit.
