# Little Camp of Horrors 2026 — Master Production Plan

## Phase 1 — Lock the Event (Aug 17–23)

### Jonathan + Shannette
- Lock Saturday schedule from breakfast through after-dark programming.
- Decide what is structured programming vs intentionally free time.
- Lock Friday arrival experience and Sunday closeout.
- Define family programming vs adult-only late-night programming.

### Jonathan
- Finalize ticket/package structure.
- Define required Eventbrite/app questions.
- Confirm venue details affecting field use, dining hall, setup access, haunted experience, power, fires, quiet hours, and equipment.
- Build master event budget framework.

### Shannette
- Finalize meal menu for Friday dinner, Saturday breakfast/lunch/dinner, and Sunday breakfast.
- Define food service windows and prep approach.
- Identify food equipment and helper requirements.

### Delegate / recruit
- Haunted Experience Lead
- Build & Decor Lead
- Activities Lead
- Volunteer Coordinator
- Operations/Setup support

## Phase 2 — Design & Source (Aug 24–Sep 6)

### Haunted Experience
- Confirm walking vs riding format.
- Map route and entrance/exit.
- Determine number of scenes/scares.
- Set target run time per group.
- Determine people per group and hourly capacity.
- Define scare level / PG-13 boundaries.
- Create materials, props, lighting, sound, costume, and actor lists.
- Identify build dates and testing date.

### Site & Decor
- Create site map for check-in, dining, main gathering, games, glamping, campsites, trick-or-treat route, haunted entrance/exit, storage, staff staging, and emergency access.
- Define entrance/signage plan.
- Define photo moment/photo area.
- Define cemetery/large set pieces and decor zones.
- Create material and purchase list.

### Activities
- Select Saturday field games.
- Create passive morning activities requiring little/no staffing.
- Define scavenger hunt or similar self-guided activity.
- Define campsite decorating contest categories and rules.
- Define official judging and People's Choice process.
- Identify prizes.

### Operations
- Inventory owned equipment.
- Identify missing/rental items.
- Test lights, extension cords, sound, cooking equipment, tents, canopies, and power equipment.
- Create initial load/transport plan.

## Phase 3 — Build, Recruit & Market (Sep 7–Oct 4)

- Build major haunted/set pieces.
- Purchase long-lead materials.
- Recruit scare actors and trail staff.
- Recruit food helpers.
- Recruit check-in and setup/breakdown volunteers.
- Recruit activity support.
- Confirm judges.
- Launch feature-by-feature marketing campaign.
- Publish food/ticket inclusions.
- Publish tent/campsite contest information.
- Publish haunted experience teaser.
- Collect trail participation intent through ticket flow.
- Track ticket sales and capacity against food and haunted throughput.

## Phase 4 — Test & Finalize (Oct 5–18)

- Run haunted experience walkthrough/test.
- Test lighting and sound under nighttime conditions.
- Confirm group capacity and throughput.
- Finalize volunteer roster and shifts.
- Finalize attendee communications.
- Finalize site map.
- Finalize menus and food quantities based on sales.
- Finalize wristband/package identification system.
- Confirm waivers/check-in backup process.
- Label event equipment by zone.
- Create zone-specific setup checklists.

## Phase 5 — Production Readiness (Oct 19–28)

- Freeze nonessential new ideas.
- Finish all build work.
- Purchase final consumables and food according to purchasing schedule.
- Print signage, schedules, rosters, maps, judging sheets, emergency info, and backup check-in materials.
- Charge/test electronics and batteries.
- Pack equipment by deployment zone, not randomly.
- Confirm every delegated lead understands outcome, supplies, schedule, and helpers.
- Conduct final organizer review.

## Thursday, Oct 29 — Full Setup Day

Goal: **Guest-ready before leaving setup mode.**

- Arrive early.
- Set Jonathan/Shannette campsites.
- Set all glamping packages.
- Build/install haunted experience.
- Set dining and food service areas.
- Set main gathering/canopy area.
- Set games/passive activity stations.
- Install decor, entrance, signage, lighting, and photo area.
- Set check-in area and materials.
- Test power, lighting, sound, trail flow, and communications.
- Perform missing-item/store run Thursday, not Friday.
- Final walk-through before end of day.

## Friday, Oct 30 — Guest Arrival

- Event is already set.
- Check in guests through app/Eventbrite with manual backup.
- Verify waivers.
- Issue wristbands/package identifiers.
- Direct guests to campsites/glamping.
- Dinner around 6:30 PM.
- Meet/greet, games, karaoke, popcorn/light entertainment.

## Saturday, Oct 31 — Main Event Day

Current skeleton:
- 8:00–10:00 AM breakfast
- 10:00 AM–12:30 PM relaxed camp morning / passive activities / decorating
- 12:30–2:30 PM lunch window
- 2:30–4:30 PM structured field games / group activity block
- 4:30–6:00 PM costume, campsite, dinner, and haunted-experience prep/reset
- 6:00 PM dinner
- Nightfall: trick-or-treating + campsite judging
- After judging begins/ends: haunted experience rotations
- Main camp remains active with Halloween party, music, snacks, games, campfire, and social activities
- Tentative 10:00 PM onward: adult-focused Camp After Dark

## Sunday, Nov 1

- Breakfast.
- Begin organized breakdown and packing.
- Inventory equipment before departure.
- Load by category/zone.
- Final site sweep.
- Departure without unnecessary rush.

## Decision rule

Any new idea must have an owner, budget impact, deadline, and place in the schedule. Otherwise it goes into the 2027 idea backlog.
