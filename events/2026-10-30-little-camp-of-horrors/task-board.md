## Rule for delegation

A delegated task must identify:
1. The outcome expected.
2. Who owns it.
3. When it is due.
4. What budget/materials are available.
5. What decisions require Jonathan/Shannette approval.

Do not delegate with “help with X.” Delegate a result.
